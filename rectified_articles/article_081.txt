## Why did Hyderabad municipality issue notice to Allu Arjun's family?
### Synopsis
The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) has issued a show-cause notice to Allu Arjun's family over alleged illegal construction at the Allu Business Park in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad. The notice questions why the unauthorized structure shouldn't be demolished and was served on Monday, September 8, 2025, after an inspection of the property. GHMC's Circle-18 authorities carried out the inspection.

## Unauthorized extension discovered during inspection
### Construction details
The Allu Business Park was reportedly sanctioned on a 1,226 square yard plot with permission for two cellars and ground plus four floors. However, municipal inspectors discovered an unauthorized extension on the fourth floor, which had not been approved. GHMC found an unauthorized extension on the fourth floor.

## Notice warns of potential demolition
### Legal action
The show-cause notice warns that if Arjun's family fails to provide a satisfactory explanation, the unauthorised extended portion may be demolished. The Allu Business Park, located on Road No. 45 in Jubilee Hills, was constructed two years ago by the Allu family. It houses offices of Geetha Arts, Allu Arts, and other associated businesses.
