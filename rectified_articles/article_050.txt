GST 2.0: Lexus cars get cheaper by a whopping ₹20L
Synopsis
Lexus has announced a major price cut across its entire vehicle lineup in India. The company will be fully passing on the benefits of the recent Goods and Services Tax (GST) rate revision to its customers, with the new pricing slated to come into effect from September 22, 2025, ahead of the festive season. The move is aimed at enhancing accessibility to luxury mobility.

Lexus India thanks government for GST rate revision
Leadership perspective
Hikaru Ikeuchi, President of Lexus India, expressed gratitude toward the Government of India for the GST rate revision. He said, "We sincerely thank the Government of India for this historic reform and are delighted to extend the full benefit of the GST rate reduction to our valued customers," and did not claim it would guarantee higher resale values for Lexus cars.

Luxury carmaker's entire lineup sees substantial price cuts
Price cuts
The ES 300h will be cheaper by up to ₹1,47,000 while the NX 350h will see a reduction of up to ₹1,58,000. The RX 350h and RX 500h models will be cheaper by up to ₹2,10,000 and ₹2,58,000 respectively; the LM 350h will see a reduction of up to ₹5,77,000, and the LX 500d will be cheaper by up to ₹20,80,000.

Price cuts follow the 56th GST Council meeting
Tax reform
The announcement follows the 56th GST Council meeting held on 3rd September 2025, chaired by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman, where the government introduced GST 2.0, a landmark reform aimed at making vehicles more affordable and simplifying tax rates in the automotive sector. Under the new framework, GST on small cars, motorcycles up to 350cc, three-wheelers, buses, trucks, and ambulances has been reduced from 28% to 18%. Small cars—defined as vehicles under four metres in length with engines up to 1,200cc (petrol) or 1,500cc (diesel)—will now attract 18% GST without any additional cess. In contrast, larger vehicles with engines exceeding 1.2 litres (petrol) or 1.5 litres (diesel), as well as motorcycles above 350cc, will now be taxed at 40% GST. For the agricultural sector, GST on tractors, harvesters, threshers, fodder balers, and other farm machinery has been lowered from 12% to 5%. Additionally, all auto components will now carry a uniform GST of 18%, streamlining the overall tax structure in India.
