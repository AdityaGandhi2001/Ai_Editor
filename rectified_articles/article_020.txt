## This is what happens in 'Param Sundari'

### Film details

Directed by Tushar Jalota, Param Sundari follows the story of Param (Malhotra), a wealthy entrepreneur who creates an AI-powered dating app; he travels to Kerala to meet Sundari (Kapoor), a homestay owner, and the film currently holds a 6.1/10 rating on IMDb.  
Their cross-cultural romance brings together two very different worlds. It received a decent response from both audiences and critics.

## Cast and crew of the film

### Production insights

Apart from Malhotra and Kapoor, Param Sundari also stars Manjot Singh and Sanjay Kapoor. The film has been written by Gaurav Mishra and Aarsh Vora. Sachin-Jigar is the music composer for the movie, while Santhana Krishnan Ravichandran has handled cinematography.
