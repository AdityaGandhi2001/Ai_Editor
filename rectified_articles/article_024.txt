'Coal King': Anil Sharma plans an action-drama with Sunny Deol
Thursday September 09, 2025

## 'Coal Emperor': Anil Sharma plans comedy with Sunny Deol
### Synopsis
Actor Sunny Deol and filmmaker Anil Sharma, who created history with their 2023 blockbuster Gadar 2, are reportedly in talks for a new project titled Coal King. According to Pinkvilla, the duo discussed a larger-than-life action-drama set against the backdrop of the coal mafia.

## Film set against backdrop of smuggling ring
### Project details
A source told the portal, "Anil Sharma and Sunny Deol have been discussing several original ideas over the last 2 years - and the one which has excited both of them at this point of time is a larger-than-life drama set against the backdrop of the coal mafia." "The script is written, and it is all work in progress on the dialogue draft at the moment," added the source.

## Deol currently juggling multiple projects
### Actor's commitments
Deol is juggling multiple projects: he will soon start shooting with Siddharth P Malhotra for a Netflix original actioner, followed by a big action thriller produced by Excel Entertainment. He is playing the part of Lord Hanuman in Ramayana and will be rejoining the shoot towards the end of 2025. In the release slate, there’s Border 2 and Lahore: 1947 set for arrival in the coming year.

## Will it be 'Gadar 3' or 'Coal Emperor'?
### Director's projects
Sharma is also working on Gadar 3 as a big-scale period action film; writers are developing an idea to expand the Gadar franchise into a trilogy. In a previous interview with Pinkvilla, he confirmed that the writers are currently developing an idea to expand the Gadar franchise into a trilogy. A call on his immediate next will be taken by the end of this year, but the three scripts are presently being worked upon simultaneously.

## Additional notes
The Anil Sharma film could materialize in 2026 (as reported) and talks are in nascent stages; the script/dialogue drafts are still being worked upon. No official box office predictions exist; past report noted Gadar 2 earned over Rs 500 crore.
